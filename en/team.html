Our camp management

bilder/team-1.jpg

“Every child can be just who they are with us. That is exactly what makes our groups so special.”

Lena,Group leader

Lena

Lena runs the summer camp and is the contact for all questions about it. She has been with the KjG since childhood and knows every corner of the campsite. She loves cooking over the campfire and telling stories that captivate even the youngest.

bilder/team-3.jpg

Jonas

Jonas takes care of the organisation on site: pitching tents, shopping and the schedule. He makes sure everything runs smoothly so that children and parents can concentrate on what matters, an unforgettable time.

bilder/team-4.jpg

Sophie

Sophie is in charge of the programme. She thinks up games, competitions and evening activities and makes sure there is something for every age. If you have questions about the daily schedule, she is the right person.

Our planning team

bilder/team-5.jpg

Max

Max plans the KjG year: dates, rooms and arrangements with the parish. He is the person to ask when you want to know when an activity takes place, and he answers parents’ questions by email.

bilder/team-6.jpg

Hanna

Hanna supports the group leaders and organises training, for example on child protection. It is important to her that every child is safe and well looked after.

bilder/team-7.jpg

Felix

Felix takes care of equipment, technology and photos. Without him there would be no tents, no projector and no lovely memories. We only publish photos if the parents have agreed.

Our parish liaison

bilder/team-8.jpg

Anna

Anna supports the KjG on behalf of the parish. She helps us with rooms, permits and questions about faith, so that we can concentrate fully on the children.

bilder/team-9.jpg

Thomas

Thomas is the pastoral contact for all concerns that go beyond organisation. He listens when there are worries and supports parents and children with personal questions.
