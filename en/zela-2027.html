Better together!

Bringing friends pays off! This year there is a special offer: anyone who brings along a friend who has never been to one of our camps before saves €20 on the participation fee!

The online registration form has a field where you can enter the name of the friend you are bringing along.

Already 14 years old?

All children who are already 14 at the time of the camp can come along as so-called “Latees”. This means they cycle to the campsite together and start the camp in a slightly different way. We will announce more information and details at a later date.
