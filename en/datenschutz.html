Photos are taken at our events. We only publish them on this website and on our social media channels if the person shown or their parents or guardians have given their consent. Consent can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future by email to kjg.mainzgonsenheim@gmail.com; we will then remove the images concerned promptly.

On this website we link to our profiles on Instagram and Facebook (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland).
